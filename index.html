How To Play

🐵
Monkey Cannon: Drag on screen to aim the trajectory dotted line. Release to fire or tap the FIRE! 🐵 button.
🎯
Match-3 Popping: Connect 3 or more of the same animal to clear them from the canopy and score points.
🍂
Cascading Drops: Cut off anchor roots to drop disconnected animals into the basket for massive bonus points!
🥥
Coconut Bombs: Match next to a coconut bomb to blast surrounding pieces.
⭐
Rainbow Wildcard: Rainbow stars match with ANY animal team!
🪨
Stone Obstacles: Cannot be matched directly. Drop them by severing the animals supporting them.
❄️
Freeze Power-Up: Randomly drops during gameplay. Shoot it to completely freeze canopy movement for 5 seconds!
⏳
Slow Motion Power-Up: Randomly drops during gameplay. Shoot it to reduce canopy descent speed by 75% for 5 seconds!
